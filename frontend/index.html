Pathology & Impact

Understanding Bovine Mastitis in Cows & Buffaloes

Mastitis is the inflammation of the mammary gland and udder tissue, predominantly triggered by bacterial infection (e.g., Staphylococcus aureus, Streptococcus uberis, and E. coli).

🦠

Subclinical vs Clinical Mastitis

Subclinical Mastitis has no visible swelling or abnormalities in milk, yet causes massive yield drops and elevated Somatic Cell Counts (SCC). Clinical Mastitis displays painful udder swelling, hardness, clots/flakes in milk, and severe distress. Vetronix catches subclinical cases days before clinical symptoms develop.

📉

Severe Economic Loss in India

Mastitis inflicts an annual loss exceeding ₹6,053 Crores ($800M) in India's dairy sector. Farmers suffer from 15-40% reduced milk yields, milk rejection at dairy collection centers due to poor microbial quality, high veterinary bills, and premature culling of high-yielding cattle.

⚡

How Multisensors Predict Mastitis

When mastitis pathogens damage udder secretory epithelial cells, the blood-milk barrier breaks down. Sodium (Na+) and Chloride (Cl-) ions rush from blood into milk, sharply elevating Electrical Conductivity. Localized inflammation causes a temperature spike (>39.5°C), and milk yield declines.

💡

The Vetronix Dual-Stage AI Advantage

While conventional screening relies on subjective visual checks or lab tests like CMT that take days, Vetronix uses an integrated multisensor probe (ESP32 + TDS Meter + DS18B20) for instant physicochemical milk evaluation (Model 1). If risk is elevated, an ESP32-CAM Teat Computer Vision Model (Model 2) inspects udder redness, erythema, and teat condition, synthesising both into actionable veterinary clinical advice.

IoT Device Architecture

Vetronix Multisensor Hardware Components

Engineered for durable, farm-grade use with real-time ADC signal conditioning, waterproof thermometry, and edge computer vision.

Brain Unit

ESP32 Microcontroller

High-performance dual-core Xtensa 32-bit LX6 MCU operating up to 240 MHz. Features integrated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE 4.2. Collects ADC telemetry from milk sensors, performs on-chip calibration, and transmits data to the Vetronix cloud dashboard.

  • Clock Speed: 240 MHz Dual-Core
  • Wireless: 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi & BLE
  • ADC: 12-bit SAR ADC (Up to 18 Channels)
  • Operating Temp: -40°C to +125°C
Electrolyte Sensor

TDS Meter 1.0 (Conductivity)

Measures Total Dissolved Solids and electrical conductivity of fresh milk in parts per million (ppm). In mastitic milk, increased Na+ and Cl- ion concentration causes higher conductivity. Vetronix converts this reading directly to milliSiemens per centimeter (mS/cm).

  • Measurement Range: 0 ~ 1000 ppm
  • Accuracy: ±10% F.S. (25°C)
  • Operating Voltage: 3.3V ~ 5.5V
  • Output Signal: 0 ~ 2.3V Analog
Thermal Probe

Temperature Sensor (DS18B20)

High-precision, food-grade waterproof digital temperature sensor encased in a stainless steel probe tube. Detects micro-temperature variations in milk immediately upon milking, flagging localized udder hyperthermia caused by inflammatory immune response.

  • Range: -55°C to +125°C
  • Accuracy: ±0.5°C (-10°C to +85°C)
  • Communication: 1-Wire Digital Bus
  • Probe: Food-Grade Stainless Steel
AI Vision Camera

ESP32-CAM (Vision Sensor)

Compact camera development module with an OV2640 2-megapixel image sensor and onboard flash LED. Captures high-definition imagery of cattle teats and udder quarters, feeding the image into Model 2 to detect erythema, swelling, and lesions.

  • Sensor: OV2640 2MP Camera
  • Resolution: UXGA (1600x1200)
  • Flash: High-Luminance Onboard LED
  • RAM: 520 KB SRAM + 4 MB PSRAM

Conductivity Conversion Formula

Milk Conductivity (mS/cm) = TDS (ppm) ÷ 500

The TDS Meter 1.0 outputs Total Dissolved Solids in parts per million (ppm). Vetronix converts the reading to milk electrical conductivity using TDS (ppm) ÷ 500 for the trained Random Forest Classifier.
